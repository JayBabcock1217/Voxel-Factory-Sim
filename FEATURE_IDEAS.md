# Feature Ideas for Factory Sim

Researched from Factorio, Minecraft, 7 Days to Die, and other survival/factory games (Nov 2026).

---

## Factory / Automation Features (Factorio-inspired)

1. **Circuit Network / Logic System**
   - Red/green wires that carry signals between machines
   - Combinators (arithmetic, logic gates) to control machine behavior
   - Enables automated production line switching (e.g., switch crafting recipes based on inventory levels)
   - Factorio's most iconic feature — huge depth potential

2. **Conveyor Belt Upgrades & Variants**
   - Speed upgrades for existing conveyors
   - Underground belts (pass through blocks)
   - Splitters (fork items between two belts)
   - Filter inserters (only take specific items)

3. **Power Generation System**
   - Solar panels (day/night cycle dependency)
   - Wind turbines
   - Nuclear reactors (fuel rods, cooling)
   - Batteries for energy storage
   - Power grid that machines draw from

4. **Fluid / Liquid System**
   - Pipes for transporting liquids
   - Tanks for storage
   - Refineries that process raw materials into fluids (e.g., oil → fuel)
   - Steam engines / turbines

5. **Robotics / Auto-Construction**
   - Construction robots that place items from blueprints
   - Logistic robots that fetch/deliver items to machines
   - Robot charging stations
   - Factorio's "logistic network" — machines request items, robots deliver them

6. **Quality / Rarity System**
   - Items have quality tiers (normal, fine, excellent, masterpiece)
   - Higher quality items produce more output or work faster
   - Quality plugins for machines
   - Adds depth to progression

7. **Recycling / Trash System**
   - Machines produce byproducts/trash
   - Recyclers convert trash back into raw materials
   - Factorio's "recycler" mod concept

8. **Blueprint System**
   - Copy/paste machine layouts
   - Blueprint book for saving multiple layouts
   - Ghost items (semi-transparent placeholders showing where to place)
   - Deconstruction planner

## Survival Features (7 Days to Die / Minecraft-inspired)

9. **Hunger / Thirst / Health System**
   - Food and water bars under health
   - Different food types give different stats
   - Hunger affects movement speed, combat ability
   - 7 Days to Die just added new food/water bars with stats

10. **Smell / Noise System**
    - Raw meat and food odors attract zombies
    - Gunshots attract enemies from far away
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just added a smell system — very popular
    - Could tie into your zombie/hive system

11. **Day/Night Cycle with Weather**
    - Dynamic day/night cycle
    - Weather (rain, snow, storms)
    - Night = increased enemy spawns
    - 7 Days to Die has biome-specific weather
    - Minecraft has day/night with mob spawning changes

12. **Building / Placement System**
    - Place walls, floors, doors, windows
    - Blueprint system for saving/loading bases
    - 7 Days to Die has voxel building
    - Could tie into your existing machine placement

13. **Vehicle System**
    - Craftable vehicles (cars, trucks)
    - Storage improvements
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just added vehicle storage mods
    - Could be a way to traverse your voxel world faster

14. **Ammunition Variety**
    - Different ammo types (standard, armor-piercing, hollow point)
    - Ammo affects damage, range, penetration
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just added ammo type benefits
    - You already have per-hotbar-slot ammo — could expand this

15. **Clothing / Armor Tiers**
    - Clothing in tiers (tattered, patchwork, thermal)
    - Armor insulation for temperature resistance
    - Cosmetic skins
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just added clothing tiers

## World / Exploration Features (Minecraft-inspired)

16. **Biomes with Unique Resources**
    - Different biomes have unique resources (cactus in desert, ice in snow)
    - Biome-specific enemies
    - 7 Days to Die has biome-specific zombie variants
    - Could add: desert (cactus, sand), snow (ice, snow), forest (trees, mushrooms)

17. **Underground Caves & Mining**
    - Procedural cave generation
    - Ore veins (coal, iron, copper, gold)
    - You already have ore materials — could tie them to underground mining

18. **Structures / POIs (Points of Interest)**
    - Abandoned camps, ruins, hives
    - Loot chests with tiered loot
    - 7 Days to Die has POIs with themed loot bags
    - Could tie into your existing hive system

19. **Water System**
    - Water sources (rivers, lakes)
    - Empty jars for water collection
    - Dew collectors
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just reintroduced empty jars

20. **Tree Growth / Farming**
    - Trees grow over time
    - Plant crops, harvest after growth
    - Factorio's "agricultural tower" concept
    - Could tie into your existing tree materials

## UI / QoL Features

21. **In-Game Journal / Codex**
    - Log discovered items, enemies, recipes
    - Expands over time as player discovers new things
    - Under a Rock has a "Materia" journal system

22. **Quest / Objective System**
    - Main quests with objectives
    - Side quests with rewards
    - 7 Days to Die has quest improvements in V2.5
    - Could guide player progression

23. **Character / Inventory UI Overhaul**
    - Rotatable character preview
    - Equipment slots (head, chest, legs, feet)
    - 7 Days to Die V2.5 just added a new character window rendering

24. **Multiplayer**
    - Co-op play (up to 5 players)
    - Shared inventory / storage
    - Guardians of the Wild Sky has co-op
    - Big feature but high impact

25. **Modding / Data-Driven Content**
    - Expose game data as JSON/XML
    - Allow custom items, enemies, biomes
    - Factorio's modding API is huge
    - Could be a long-term goal

---

## Top 5 Recommendations (Highest Impact, Lowest Effort)

1. **Circuit Network / Logic System** — Factorio's most iconic feature, huge depth, builds on your existing machine system
2. **Hunger / Thirst / Health System** — Core survival mechanic, ties into your existing inventory, high player engagement
3. **Biomes with Unique Resources** — Adds exploration incentive, builds on your existing voxel world, low effort
4. **Blueprint System** — QoL feature, saves player time, builds on your existing machine placement
5. **Day/Night Cycle with Weather** — Atmospheric, ties into enemy spawning, builds on your existing world
